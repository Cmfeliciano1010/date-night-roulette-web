A NOTE FROM DATE NIGHT ROULETTE

A spin, a spark, a plan.

This working web prototype chooses from a built-in library. Your budget, setting, energy and time filter the ideas before each random spin.

Budgets are estimates for two people in US dollars. At-home free ideas assume you use things you already own. Travel costs are extra. Check prices, hours, weather and accessibility before heading out.

Your preferences and favorites stay in this browser’s local storage. We don’t request GPS or send your city to a date-generation service. Clear this browser’s site data to remove your saved information. The hosting provider may process standard access logs.

Opening “Find a spot” sends the search and any city you entered to Google Maps in a new tab, under Google’s privacy terms. Local venue availability is not verified.

Free to use, with no ads or subscriptions. This is a prototype; an Android Play Store release is a separate next step.
